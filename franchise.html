FAQs

Franchise Questions

What franchise models are available?

MAU'S GLOBAL offers three franchise models to enquire about: Micro Store, Mini Store and Large Store.

How can I apply for a franchise?

Complete the franchise enquiry form on this page. Our team can contact you to discuss your requirements and available options.

What are the investment requirements?

Investment requirements depend on the selected store model and applicable franchise terms. Contact our team for verified details.

Can I enquire if I do not have a business location?

Yes. You can submit an enquiry even if you are still searching for a suitable business location.

Does submitting an enquiry guarantee a franchise?

No. An enquiry is only an expression of interest. Franchise approval is subject to review, eligibility and a separate agreement.
